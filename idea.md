## Pending Ideas

### 1. Sell Tracking *(highest priority — biggest gap)*
- Record when you sell a stock: date, quantity sold, sell price
- Calculate realized P&L per trade (sell value − cost basis)
- Show holding period per trade
- Separate "closed positions" from active holdings
- Capital gains summary: short-term vs long-term

### 2. Portfolio Snapshot Timeline
- Save a snapshot of your portfolio value on any given day
- Plot your net worth growth over months/years
- Compare snapshots: "6 months ago vs today"
- Great for tracking progress without relying on live prices

### 3. Goal Tracker
- Set a target corpus (e.g. ₹50L by 2030)
- App shows: current value, required monthly SIP to hit goal, projected date at current growth rate
- Visual progress bar toward the goal
- Multiple goals (retirement, house, education)

### 4. SIP Simulator
- Pick any stock or index, set a monthly amount and start date
- Simulate what would have happened if you SIP'd into it
- Compare: your actual investment vs simulated SIP in same stock
- Shows average cost, XIRR, total invested vs current value

### 5. Opportunity Cost Calculator
- Pick an alternative (gold, Nifty, FD at 7%)
- App shows: "If you had put ₹X into Y instead, you'd have ₹Z"
- Side-by-side: your portfolio return vs the alternative
- Helps answer "was this stock worth it?"

### 6. Tax Estimator *(discussed, deferred)*
- STCG (< 1 year): 15% flat
- LTCG (> 1 year): 10% above ₹1L
- Show estimated tax liability on unrealized gains if sold today
- Highlight which stocks cross the 1-year mark soon
